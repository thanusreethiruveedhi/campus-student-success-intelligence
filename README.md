# campus-student-success-intelligence
An AI-powered Business Intelligence system for analyzing campus resources, student engagement, academic performance, and identifying students who may need academic support.
## Business Questions

### Academic Performance
1. What is the overall average CGPA?
2. Which branch has the highest average CGPA?
3. Is attendance associated with CGPA?
4. Is previous CGPA associated with final CGPA?
5. Is assignment performance associated with final CGPA?

### Student Engagement
6. Is LMS activity associated with academic performance?
7. Is library usage associated with academic performance?
8. Are projects and certifications associated with stronger academic performance?
9. How does internship experience vary across academic years?

### Risk Analysis
10. How many students are classified as high risk?
11. Which branches have the highest proportion of high-risk students?
12. What characteristics are common among high-risk students?
13. Are backlogs associated with higher academic risk?
14. Is low attendance associated with higher risk?

### Institutional Questions
15. Which factors are most strongly associated with student success?
16. Which student groups may require additional academic support?
17. Which campus resources appear underused?
18. What data-backed interventions could improve student outcomes?
## Project Workflow

The project follows a complete Business Intelligence workflow:

1. Raw Data Collection
2. Data Cleaning
3. Data Understanding
4. Exploratory Data Analysis
5. Business Question Analysis
6. Insight Generation
7. Predictive Analytics
8. Student Risk Classification
9. Interactive BI Dashboard
10. Data-Driven Recommendations
## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze academic performance,
student engagement, and campus resource usage to identify patterns
associated with student success and identify students who may require
additional academic support.

The system combines Business Intelligence, Exploratory Data Analysis,
Machine Learning, and interactive visualization into a single platform.
